OrbitRing derives bundled vector geometry from the official variable font.
The fixed instance uses FILL=0, GRAD=0, opsz=24, wght=400.
Coordinates are scaled and Y-flipped into a 24 by 24 viewport.
Alias names sharing a codepoint are consolidated into a single entry.
Japanese labels, search vocabulary, and primary categories were added by OrbitRing.
The assets are embedded locally; the application does not fetch icons at runtime.

fontTools 4.67.0 was used only during asset preparation and is not required or
distributed as an OrbitRing runtime dependency.
